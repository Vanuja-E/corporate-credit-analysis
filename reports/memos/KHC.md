# The Kraft Heinz Company (KHC)

Prepared by Vanuja Ekanayake, BBA Finance candidate, Baruch College Zicklin School of Business.

Recommendation: **Weak**. Composite 1.50 on a 1.00 to 4.00 scale for the fiscal year ended 2025-12-27.

Kraft Heinz manufactures and sells packaged foods.
The fiscal 2025 Form 10-K states that long-term debt is rated BBB with a stable outlook by S&P Global Ratings, BBB with a rating watch negative outlook by Fitch Ratings, and Baa2 with ratings under review for downgrade by Moody's Investor Services, Inc. Operating income is after impairment losses. This model does not add those losses back to EBITDA. Cash from operations is shown separately so a non-cash charge is not read as a cash shortfall.

Figures are the latest Form 10-K amounts on file for each period end. The fiscal 2025 column used in this memo is taken from https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1637459/000163745926000009/khc-20251227.htm.

## Limits of this memo

Total debt excludes operating leases and, where the issuer's debt tags contain them, finance leases.
Net debt does not deduct short-term investments.
EBITDA is operating income plus depreciation and amortization. It does not add back impairments, stock-based compensation, or restructuring.
This is not a rating-agency opinion and it is not a commitment to lend.
