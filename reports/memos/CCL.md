Prepared by Vanuja Ekanayake, BBA Finance candidate, Baruch College Zicklin School of Business.

Recommendation: **Weak**. Composite 1.70 on a 1.00 to 4.00 scale for the fiscal year ended 2025-11-30.

Carnival operates cruise lines.
Total debt is the carrying amount of current and noncurrent long-term debt. The principal maturity table in the 10-K is higher than that carrying amount by unamortized discounts and issuance costs; the model uses the carrying amount. Operating lease liabilities are excluded. The interest line is interest expense, net of capitalized interest. No agency rating is stated in this note.

Figures are the latest Form 10-K amounts on file for each period end. The fiscal 2025 column used in this memo is taken from https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/815097/000081509726000007/ccl-20251130.htm.

## Limits of this memo

Total debt excludes operating leases and, where the issuer's debt tags contain them, finance leases.
Net debt does not deduct short-term investments.
EBITDA is operating income plus depreciation and amortization. It does not add back impairments, stock-based compensation, or restructuring.
This is not a rating-agency opinion and it is not a commitment to lend.
