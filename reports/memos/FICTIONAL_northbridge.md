# FICTIONAL Northbridge Precision Components, LLC

FICTIONAL borrower. This company is not real, is not an SEC registrant, and is not a bank client.
Every borrower figure is an assumption. The schedule is a forecast. The downside cases are simulated.

Facility requested: Senior secured first-lien term loan.
Amount requested: $40,000,000.
Recommended commitment: $25,900,000.
Binding sizing constraint: minimum DSCR.

Cash flow available for debt service is EBITDA minus maintenance capex minus cash taxes.
EBITDA is operating income plus depreciation and amortization, $14.00 million.
Cash flow available for debt service is $10.00 million.
Opening leverage on the recommended loan is 1.85x against a 3.50x covenant.
Year-1 DSCR is 1.35x against a 1.25x covenant.
Year-1 interest coverage is 6.36x against a 2.50x covenant.
Leverage headroom is 1.65x. DSCR headroom is 0.10x.

The requested $40.00 million would produce a year-1 DSCR of 0.88x, which is below the 1.25x minimum. The request is not approved at that amount.

The debt roll-forward is opening principal plus draws minus principal repayments. It equals the closing principal in every period, and the final balance is zero.

Downside cases hold maintenance capex and cash taxes at the base assumption. That is conservative when EBITDA falls, because cash taxes are not reduced. Year 1 is the tightest point on a level-principal loan.
