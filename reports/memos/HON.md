Recommendation: **Elevated Risk**. Composite 2.20 on a 1.00 to 4.00 scale for the fiscal year ended 2025-12-31.

Honeywell is a diversified industrial company.
The fiscal 2025 Form 10-K discloses long-term ratings of A (S&P), A (Fitch), and A2 (Moody's), with the outlook row reading Watch Negative, Watch Negative, and Stable in that column order. Those are the issuer's disclosures, not a result of this scorecard. Finance lease liabilities sit inside the long-term debt lines and are subtracted. Operating leases are not added. Consolidated totals include the effects of portfolio changes described in the 10-K.

Figures are the latest Form 10-K amounts on file for each period end. The fiscal 2025 column used in this memo is taken from https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/773840/000077384026000013/hon-20251231.htm.

## Limits of this memo

Total debt excludes operating leases and, where the issuer's debt tags contain them, finance leases.
Net debt does not deduct short-term investments.
EBITDA is operating income plus depreciation and amortization. It does not add back impairments, stock-based compensation, or restructuring.
This is not a rating-agency opinion and it is not a commitment to lend.
