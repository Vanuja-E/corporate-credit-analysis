# PepsiCo, Inc. (PEP)

Prepared by Vanuja Ekanayake, BBA Finance candidate, Baruch College Zicklin School of Business.

Recommendation: **Elevated Risk**. Composite 2.50 on a 1.00 to 4.00 scale for the fiscal year ended 2025-12-27.

PepsiCo produces beverages and convenient foods.
The income-statement interest tag for this issuer is the line "net interest expense and other." When cash interest paid is materially higher, coverage uses cash interest paid and both amounts are shown. A current ratio below 1 is structural: current liabilities include commercial paper and current debt maturities. The scorecard records that result at its published weight.

Figures are the latest Form 10-K amounts on file for each period end. The fiscal 2025 column used in this memo is taken from https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/77476/000007747626000007/pep-20251227.htm.

## Limits of this memo

Total debt excludes operating leases and, where the issuer's debt tags contain them, finance leases.
Net debt does not deduct short-term investments.
EBITDA is operating income plus depreciation and amortization. It does not add back impairments, stock-based compensation, or restructuring.
This is not a rating-agency opinion and it is not a commitment to lend.
